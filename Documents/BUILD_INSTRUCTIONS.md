Build Instructions — Rev D Draft

1. Confirm competition class, envelope, mass, battery, radio, safety, and weapon requirements.
2. Select commercial drivetrain hardware and verify actual mounting patterns.
3. Engineering-review materials, thicknesses, fasteners, welds, load cases, fatigue, and safety factors.
4. Verify every mating hole and interface in AutoCAD.
5. Generate CAM toolpaths and inspect a first article.
6. Assemble chassis, drivetrain, service panels, electronics, battery containment, and independent power isolation.
7. Keep any weapon module separate until independently engineered and approved.
8. Perform controlled no-weapon mobility and failsafe testing.
9. Record as-built dimensions, mass, material certificates, inspection results, wiring revision, and test results.
